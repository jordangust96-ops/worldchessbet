## Contest Format
- **Match format.** ChessBet offers head-to-head chess. Money matches use the entry, fee, and prize rules below. Free matches have no entry fee or cash prize.
- **Game record.** ChessBet’s server validates moves, enforces the clocks, and determines when a game ends. The server’s board position and move log are the official record.

## Player Eligibility
- **Who can play.** Paid play requires U.S. residency, verified identity, age 21 or older, physical presence in a supported location, and a verified bank account.
- **Supported states.** The current supported states are Arkansas, Colorado, Georgia, Iowa, Kansas, North Dakota, Texas, Virginia, Wisconsin, and Wyoming. This is ChessBet's access policy, not a representation of government approval or a legal determination for any state. Availability can change and remains subject to applicable law, account restrictions, and enabled payment and contest services.
- **Account limit.** Each player may maintain only one Account.
- **Location checks.** ChessBet checks location once during wallet setup, before identity verification. Later deposits use that saved result. Paid challenge creation, acceptance, and pre-match readiness require current location checks.
- **Location evidence.** ChessBet uses your network location and may consider available device or browser location evidence for match checks. A home address or bank connection does not establish physical presence. Uncertain, conflicting, or unsupported evidence can prevent paid play.
- **During play.** Players must remain in a supported location during paid play. ChessBet does not continuously track GPS location.

## Challenge Preparation and Cancellation
- **Create a money challenge.** Before ChessBet confirms a new money challenge link, it checks eligibility and reserves the creator’s Entry Amount and separate Platform Service Fee from playable funds. Sharing or viewing the link does not assign an opponent.
- **Free challenges.** Free challenges require no entry funds or paid-play location check.
- **Claiming a challenge.** The first eligible recipient with sufficient playable funds can claim an ordinary challenge, even if the creator is offline. A rematch link may be restricted to the previous opponent and expire sooner.
- **Entry and fee reservation.** Acceptance reserves the recipient's displayed entry and fee without charging the creator again. Pending deposits, pending winnings and funds already reserved for another purpose are not playable.
- **Ready to play.** Each player completes a brief Fair Play attestation and explicitly confirms readiness before the game starts. Creating or accepting a link does not record that pre-match attestation.
- **Deadlines.** An unclaimed ordinary challenge normally expires after 24 hours. After acceptance, both players have five minutes to return and confirm readiness. The match screen shows the countdown. Earlier accepted matches keep their recorded deadline.
- **Acceptance notifications.** If the creator has acceptance notifications enabled and email delivery succeeds, they receive a direct match link and the start deadline.
- **Cancellation and refunds.** Participants may cancel before the match begins its start transition; cancellation, expiry or a missed start window releases the entry amounts and fees actually reserved back to playable wallet balance through foreground or scheduled recovery. Processing may finish after the displayed deadline.
- **Interrupted requests.** An interrupted request is recovered using its existing financial operation and must not cause a second charge.
- **After the start transition.** Once the start transition or live play begins, ordinary cancellation is unavailable.
- **Which challenges these rules cover.** These creation rules apply to new public and link-only money challenges. Older challenges retain their recorded reservation terms. ChessBet shows the selected time control when you create a challenge.

## Contest Entry Amounts and Platform Service Fees

- **Prize and service fee.** The fee is separate from the entries and is never deducted from the Contest Prize. The fee schedule below covers supported amounts; the challenge interface determines which entry options are currently offered.

## Settlement of Contest Outcomes
- **Decisive result.** A decisive result credits the winner with the full combined Entry Amounts as the Contest Prize and recognizes both players' separately reserved Platform Service Fees. The prize includes the winner's own Entry Amount; it is not the winner's net profit.
- **Pending winnings.** After a decisive result, the Contest Prize is credited to pending winnings. The standard reporting window is twenty-four (24) hours from the recorded settlement.
- **Release conditions.** Release is processed automatically after the deadline when no open dispute or blocking integrity or settlement-reconciliation flag remains. Processing may occur after the displayed deadline; a report is not the only reason funds can remain held.
- **Refunds.** A draw, accepted cancellation, or administrative void returns each player's Entry Amount and Platform Service Fee actually reserved.
- **Technical issues.** A connection problem or technical complaint does not automatically void a Contest; the server result and any subsequent review determine the treatment.

## Deposit Availability and Withdrawal Processing
- **Deposit costs.** Before a deposit is submitted, the Wallet shows the wallet credit, separate deposit fee, and total bank debit. The deposit fee helps cover bank processing, verification, and standard transfer services.
- **When deposits become playable.** Settled deposits become available for paid contest play once Seamless reports Processed (Paid) and ChessBet completes the provider and applicable settlement-amount checks. Pending deposits cannot be played.
- **Request a withdrawal.** Players can request a withdrawal from Available Balance while bank processing is still in progress. ChessBet reserves the requested amount and any applicable withdrawal fee, records the request, and sends an email confirmation with an estimated arrival date.
- **When payment is sent.** The backend submits payment when the relevant account, bank, funding-source and transfer-capacity checks pass. Deposit-funded payments cannot be sent before five business days from the original deposit submission, excluding weekends and Federal Reserve banking holidays, and require a successful final provider-status check. Existing recorded processing dates are preserved. An arrival estimate can change with bank processing or additional verification and does not protect against a later bank return.
- **Withdrawal fees.** Standard withdrawals of $10 or more have no separate withdrawal fee. Withdrawing the entire Available Balance waives the fee; otherwise, a request below $10 currently has a $2.50 fee.
- **Holds carry forward.** When a contest uses funds under a deposit withdrawal hold, the full resulting Contest Prize inherits every remaining source hold. Replaying winnings in later contests, refunds, administrative adjustments, and account closure do not bypass those restrictions.
- **Mixed funding.** Mixed-funded prizes and related refunds can remain restricted until all applicable source holds are cleared; unrelated unspent funds remain separately withdrawable.
- **Independent reviews.** The existing twenty-four-hour contest reporting window and dispute or integrity reviews apply independently. A returned source deposit can require review of affected funds.

## Determining a Winner
- **How a contest ends.** A Contest can end by checkmate, resignation, timeout, or a draw.
- **Draws.** The app automatically recognizes stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule, and insufficient material through its chess engine; players can also agree to a draw.
- **Timeout rule.** For timeout decisions, ChessBet currently uses a material-count rule: the non-timed-out player has sufficient material if they have a pawn, rook, queen, or at least two bishops/knights in total.
- **Insufficient material.** A lone king, or a king with only one bishop or knight, is insufficient. If the player whose clock is still running has insufficient material under ChessBet’s material-count rule, the game is a draw.

## Disconnections and Technical Issues
- **What a missed heartbeat means.** ChessBet detects a disconnect when it stops receiving signals from a player’s device. A missed signal does not establish intentional abandonment. Taking time to think while connected does not trigger a disconnect.
- **Reconnect windows.** The countdown starts when ChessBet detects the disconnect, even if it is the other player’s turn. The displayed windows are 38 seconds for 3+0, 68 seconds for 10+0, and 98 seconds for 15+0, including an eight-second confirmation interval.
- **What happens when time runs out.** If the chess clock reaches zero first, the chess-clock timeout rule applies. If the reconnect countdown ends first, ChessBet checks that the disconnected player is still offline and their opponent is connected before awarding a disconnect win. If both players are offline, ChessBet does not award a disconnect win.
- **Clock and reports.** The reconnect countdown does not pause or add time to either clock. Report technical issues in the app. Filing a report does not automatically pause, void, or change a result.

## Fair Play Screening, Integrity Review, and Appeals
- **Post-game screening.** Completed Contests can be queued for Stockfish-powered post-game screening and rule-based behavioral checks. Screening depends on service availability and sufficient game data; queuing is not a guarantee of completed analysis.
- **Signals reviewed.** Available indicators may include engine agreement, centipawn loss, critical-position performance, move timing, focus-loss events, repeated opponents, unusual resignations, and unusual timeouts.
- **Human review.** Automated signals do not by themselves prove cheating, change a result, or impose an account penalty. Open blocking integrity or settlement-reconciliation flags can delay the automatic release of pending winnings while the matter is reviewed. A person considers the available game, technical, account, report, and dispute evidence before an enforcement decision.
- **If no violation is found.** An Integrity Review does not itself establish a violation. If no violation is found, the result and ordinary decisive-result fees stand.
- **Voids and reversals.** If a Contest is voided, both players' Entry Amounts and Platform Service Fees are returned. A reversal of a settled decisive result removes the original winner's Contest Prize, returns the original loser's Entry Amount, and leaves the remaining entry funds reserved for a separate disposition; it does not automatically award the full prize to the other player.
- **Fees after a reversal.** On a reversal, the administrator records whether the separately charged Platform Service Fees are retained or refunded. Contest entry funds are not recorded as platform fee revenue.
- **Appeals.** A participant in a resolved case with an adverse determination may submit one in-app appeal per case and supporting information. The current in-app appeal flow does not impose a seven-day cutoff. Contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} if account restrictions prevent access.

## Disputes
- **How to report.** Report a contest concern in the app within 24 hours of the recorded contest settlement. If you report from Wallet History, the associated transaction’s own 24-hour deadline also applies.
- **What ChessBet reviews.** ChessBet may review game records, server logs, automated screening records, integrity flags, player reports, dispute evidence, and other relevant information to resolve disputes.
- **Funds on hold.** An open dispute blocks the automatic release of pending winnings for that contest. Filing a report does not itself move funds or reverse a result.
