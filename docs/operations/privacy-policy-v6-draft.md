## Information We Collect
- **Account information.** We collect your name, email, username, and support communications. Base44 handles sign-in.
- **Identity and age.** Socure’s hosted flow may collect identity details, date of birth, ID images, and related evidence. ChessBet receives verification decisions, verified name and age indicators, provider references, and retained provider-report evidence.
- **Bank and payment information.** SeamlessChex (Seamless) and its hosted Plaid flow handle bank connection. You do not enter bank login credentials or full account numbers into ChessBet. We retain provider references, verification status, available bank metadata, ACH authorization and signer details, and transaction records. Bank verification is separate from Socure identity verification.

## Information Collected Automatically
- **Device, security and location data.** We collect technical information, account activity, security and session records, and IP-based location and risk data through MaxMind. If provided or permitted, device or browser location evidence can help assess eligibility and detect mismatches. These estimates can be inconclusive.
- **Gameplay.** We collect moves, clocks, move timing, focus-loss events, and operational telemetry.
- **Device fingerprinting.** ChessBet no longer generates or submits a browser fingerprint for location eligibility. Previously recorded information may remain subject to retention requirements.
- **Native iOS app.** Base44 reports that its iOS shell uses Firebase for app usage analytics and Sentry for crash and error diagnostics. Reports may include device information, installation identifiers, or a ChessBet user ID. Website cookie choices do not control native collection.

## Information Generated by the Platform
- **Platform records.** We maintain contest records, wallet and transfer records, integrity reviews, and audit and security logs.

## How We Use Information
- **Run and secure ChessBet.** Create and protect accounts, verify eligibility, and support players.
- **Process games and payments.** Verify bank authorization, reconcile transfers, run contests, and settle results.
- **Investigate concerns.** Identify potential fraud or prohibited conduct and review reports, disputes, and appeals.
- **Meet obligations.** Send permitted communications and meet applicable legal and payment-provider requirements.

## Third-Party Service Providers
- **Base44.** Hosts the app and its data, handles sign-in, and supports communications and native iOS services.
- **Socure, SeamlessChex, Plaid and MaxMind.** Socure verifies identity and age. SeamlessChex handles payments and bank authorization through its hosted Plaid flow. MaxMind supplies IP-based location and risk information.
- **Infrastructure.** We use services for financial coordination and post-game Stockfish analysis.
- **Blog images.** ChessBet serves articles through its own publishing system. External images in an article can disclose connection information to the host when loaded.
- **Optional website measurement.** Base44 visitor analytics, Google Analytics, and Meta receive information for the permitted purposes described below. Hosted identity and bank services have their own privacy notices. Each provider receives information needed for its service.

## Data Storage and Security
- **Storage and access.** ChessBet stores account, transaction, game, and review records in its application data store. We encrypt retained Socure provider reports before storage and restrict access to sensitive verification, financial, and admin records.
- **Service providers.** Infrastructure services process the data they need to provide their services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

## Account Closure and Data Retention
- **Request deletion.** In Profile, choose **Delete account and personal data**, or see [Account Deletion](/account-deletion). Support reviews and fulfills requests manually and aims to complete them within 30 days. We explain if we need more time.
- **Closure and pending activity.** Contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for help with closure or data rights. A deletion request does not itself close the account, cancel a game, move money, or lift holds. Closing an account does not accelerate pending settlement or automatically delete required records.
- **Retention.** Transaction records and relevant identity and authorization evidence have minimum two-year retention periods. Related activity can extend those periods. We can retain records longer for operations, disputes, fraud prevention, and legal or processor obligations.
- **What we delete.** We delete information we do not need to keep and explain which categories we retain and why. Other records do not have one universal deletion schedule.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies
- **Essential storage.** Cookies and browser storage support sign-in, MFA, security, gameplay, and remembering your choice. Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent account access or gameplay.
- **Your choice.** Optional website tracking is off until you opt in. Use **Cookie settings** in the public footer or Profile to change or withdraw your choice. Your choice applies to this browser and lasts up to 180 days. Another browser, cleared storage, or blocked storage can affect whether it persists.
- **Analytics.** With permission, Base44 and Google Analytics measure eligible public-page visits. Google Analytics cookies such as `_ga` and `_ga_JLHMN26FS2` are configured for up to 180 days.
- **Advertising.** With separate permission, Meta measures eligible public-page visits. Its `_fbp` cookie typically lasts 90 days. These providers can receive technical information such as IP address and browser details when contacted. Browser settings may further limit cookies.
- **Where website tracking stays off.** Optional tracking is off on signed-in, login, registration, MFA, wallet, identity and admin pages, and when a URL contains a query or fragment. ChessBet does not send Meta identity-verification events, account identifiers, or contest amounts or results.
- **Global Privacy Control and consent changes.** Global Privacy Control keeps optional website tracking off. Withdrawing permission stops future optional collection and clears accessible optional cookies. It does not erase data a provider already received.
- **iPhone and iPad.** Optional website tracking stays off, including in ChessBet’s iOS web content and on desktop-mode iPad browsers. These choices do not govern native iOS app analytics or diagnostics. A website cookie choice does not grant Apple App Tracking Transparency permission.
- **Session recording and retention.** The website blocks Base44 session recording and does not use HeyCatch browser tracking. Essential operational and security processing continues. Cookie lifetimes differ from provider retention periods. Hosted identity and bank services have their own notices.
