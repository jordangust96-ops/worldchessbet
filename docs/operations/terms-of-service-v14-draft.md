## Disconnections and Game Results
- **Connection interruptions.** ChessBet detects a disconnect when it stops receiving signals from a player’s device. A missed signal does not prove intentional abandonment. The chess clock keeps running, and taking time to think while connected does not trigger a disconnect.
- **Reconnect countdown.** The countdown starts when ChessBet detects a disconnect, even if it is the other player’s turn. The displayed windows are 38 seconds for 3+0, 68 seconds for 10+0, and 98 seconds for 15+0, including eight seconds for final confirmation. If the chess clock expires first, the chess-clock timeout rule applies. If the reconnect countdown expires first, ChessBet checks that one player is still offline and the opponent is connected before awarding a disconnect win.
- **Technical reports.** A report does not automatically pause a game, void it, or change the result. See the Official Rules for result and reporting procedures.
